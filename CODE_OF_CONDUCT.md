# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to a respectful, inclusive and evidence-conscious project community. Be constructive, credit other people's work, distinguish fact from suggestion, and welcome contributors with different disciplines and levels of technical experience.

Harassment, discriminatory language, personal attacks, deliberate misrepresentation and publication of another person's confidential research material are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Report conduct or security-sensitive concerns privately to the repository maintainer through the contact route on their GitHub profile. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject contributions and temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.
